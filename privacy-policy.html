Privacy Policy

Franko's Rainfall Recorder · last updated 23 September 2026

This app collects nothing. Your rainfall records stay on your own device, and there is no account, no server and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

The rainfall readings you enter, the names you give your properties and rain gauges, and your display preferences. All of it is written to your device's own storage by the app, and nowhere else.

What is collected about you

Nothing. Specifically, the app does not collect or transmit:

  • Personal information, contact details or your name
  • Location data — the app never asks for or uses your location
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics
  • Advertising or marketing identifiers
  • Your rainfall records themselves

There is no account to create and nothing to log in to.

Network use

The app makes no network requests. It works exactly the same with the device in flight mode.

Sharing with others

No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected. There are no third-party services, analytics providers, advertising networks or software development kits that receive data from this app.

Files you export yourself

The app can export your records as a CSV file. When you do that, you choose where the file goes using your device's own share sheet — email, a cloud drive, a messaging app, or saved to Files. That file then follows the privacy policy of wherever you sent it. The app itself never uploads it anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes every reading it holds. There is no copy anywhere else for us to delete, and no request you need to make.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, it will be updated here and the date at the top will change.
