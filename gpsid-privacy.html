Privacy Policy

GPSID Software and GPSID (Leader Products) · last updated 2 October 2026

The app keeps your livestock records on your own device. It has no account, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends something over the internet only when you ask it to.

What the app stores on your device

  • The tag numbers your GPSID reader reads, with the date, time and GPS position the reader records for each.
  • Anything you type in: session names, visual tags, weights, notes and other animal details.
  • Your settings: language, country, badge and reader settings, and which reader you last connected.
  • Files you save from the app (sessions, NLIS or NAIT files), in the place you choose.

All of this stays on your device unless you send or share it yourself.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your GPSID reader and read tag numbers from it.

When the app uses the internet

  • NLIS (Australia) — only when you press Send to NLIS or ask NLIS a question, the app sends that transaction (PICs, tag numbers, dates and the details on the form, and the NLIS user ID and password you typed) directly to the NLIS database run by Integrity Systems Company. Nothing is sent to us.
  • The GPS map — while the map page is open, map pictures are fetched from OpenStreetMap; like any website, OpenStreetMap's servers see your device's internet address. Your tag positions are drawn on your device and are not sent. In China no map pictures are fetched.
  • Open in Maps — when you press it, one position is handed to your device's maps app (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Amap).

Your NLIS password

Your NLIS password is used only to send to NLIS. It is remembered only if you tick “Remember password”, and then only in your device's own protected storage; it is forgotten if NLIS refuses it.

What is not collected

We do not collect or receive any of your data. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no account. We cannot see your sessions, your animals or your location.

Sharing

A session or file leaves your device only if you share or save it somewhere yourself, for example by email or to a cloud folder of your choosing.

Children

The app is a farm tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact
